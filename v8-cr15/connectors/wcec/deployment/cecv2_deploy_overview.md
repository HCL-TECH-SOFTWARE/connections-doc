# Deploying HCL Connections Engagement Center (WebEngine)

HCL Connections Engagement Center (WebEngine) is a Kubernetes-based platform for creating modern, rich, and highly customizable experiences within HCL Connections.

This section guides you to efficiently deploy CEC (WebEngine) on Component Pack, focusing on Helm based deployments on Kubernetes cluster.

!!! important
    Before proceeding with any installation steps, please thoroughly review your environment meets all **[System requirements](./system-requirements/sys_req_resource_alloc_size_guide.md)**.
    
## Deployment workflow

To successfully deploy HCL Connections Engagement Center (WebEngine), follow the steps outlined below in sequence. Each section provides detailed instructions around the deployment process.

- **[Pre-installation tasks](./prepare-installation/preinstall_overview.md)**

    This section guides you through configuring your infrastructure, installing prerequisite software, and gathering necessary credentials to prepare your environment for the CEC deployment.

- **[Installing and configuring HCL Connections Engagement Center (WebEngine)](./installation/install_overview.md)**

    Follow these instructions to deploy CEC onto your Kubernetes cluster using Helm.

- **[Post installation tasks](./post-installation/post_install_overview.md)**

    Perform essential configuration and verification steps to finalize your CEC deployment and ensure it is fully operational.

- **[Uninstallation](./uninstallation/cecv2_uninstall.md)**

    This section provides instructions for safely removing the CEC deployment from your environment.

-  **[Troubleshooting](./troubleshooting/troubleshooting.md)**

    Find solutions and guidance for common issues that may arise during or after the deployment of CEC.
