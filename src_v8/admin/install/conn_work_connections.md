# Surveys in HCL Connections communities {#workingwithibmconnections .concept}

You can create polls and surveys in your Connections communities when you integrate Community Surveys with Connections. When you integrate the two products, the survey widgets, Surveys and Featured Survey, are enabled in community pages.

## Community surveys { .section}

Community Surveys delivers survey components for integration with HCL Connections 6. These components include two different survey widgets for community pages. The Survey widget shows active surveys in a community page. The Featured Survey widget displays a survey or survey results.

The built-in survey designer provides a variety of different question types for you when you create a poll or survey.

Through Connections activity streams, community members can view and participate in surveys, either in the homepage or the community updates page.

-   **[Enabling Community Surveys on the Community Surveys server](../install/t_enable_comm_surveys_on_server.md)**  
You enable the Community Surveys feature by copying the files provided to the extension directory on the Community Surveys server, and then editing the Connections configuration file.
-   **[Registering the resource bundle to provide titles and descriptions for iWidget](../install/in_deploy_register_bundles_iwidget_connections.md)**  
You must copy and register the resource bundle from Community Surveys to Connections.
-   **[Enabling custom widgets for Communities](../install/in_deploy_custom_widgets_communities.md)**  
To enable the custom widgets for Communities, you must copy files to the HCL Connections server and edit the configuration file.
-   **[Deploying the survey gadget on the HCL Connections home page](../install/in_deploy_survey_connections.md)**  
To deploy the survey gadget so community members can use it, follow these steps.
